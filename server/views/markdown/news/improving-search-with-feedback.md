# Improving Search with Team Feedback

Search is central to making reuse practical. Over the past month we collected feedback
from teams across departments and used it to improve relevance.

## Improvements delivered

We have focused on practical changes that make useful content easier to find:

- better matching for common terms used by delivery teams
- clearer result snippets to explain why a match is relevant
- stronger filtering to narrow results by department and content type

## Keep the feedback coming

Your feedback directly shapes the platform. If search results are not what you expect,
please share examples so we can keep improving discoverability.
