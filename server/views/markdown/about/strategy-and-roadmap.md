# Strategy and roadmap

## Our strategy

Our strategy is to grow awareness of the library, demonstrate its impact through evidence, and increase community
participation.

We're doing this by:

* showing the library to people who deliver government services, including through demos and senior leaders
* measuring how the library is used, so we can show its impact
* making it easier to contribute, and keeping the resources in the library up to date
