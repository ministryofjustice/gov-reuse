# Vision and mission

## Our vision

Our vision is that people who design government services will reuse work efficiently and confidently, while building
a culture of collaboration and visibility.

## Our mission

Our mission is to create a library platform of high-quality, discoverable, reusable assets that people value and
trust, and that connects colleagues to each other's work.

To do this, we:

* bring government resources together in one place, so people can find what they need and reuse it
* check that search results and resources are accurate, and show who made them
* link colleagues across professions and organisations, so they can build on each other's work
* support consistent, accessible design across government, so the public get better services

## Who the library is for

The library is for anyone working on UK government services. That includes designers, researchers, policy designers
and developers in central and local government, as well as academics.
